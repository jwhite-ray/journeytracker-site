Gods on the Oxcart

NATHAN: Now the part our guest has been waiting for. Cyrus.

THE ACADEMIC: "Thus saith the LORD to his anointed, to Cyrus, whose right hand I have holden." 45:1. Anointed is mashiach, the word for Israel's anointed kings and priests. Here, and only here in the Hebrew Bible, it's given to a foreign king, and the text says twice he doesn't know Israel's God: "though thou hast not known me." Now set that beside the Cyrus Cylinder, a clay inscription written after 539.
