What the table says

Team Jensen has made the top five seven weeks out of seven. That is the whole story at the top. Jensen lost in Week 7 to Stowell by 1.36 points and still earned a point for the 122.42 score. That is the format's insurance policy, and it's why Jensen leads by two despite being fourth in points for.

Team Carvalho is 6–1 and only third. Carvalho has won six matchups but has made the top five only three times. In Week 7 its 110.32 beat Cee Deez Nuts but finished 8th, so a win earned one point when it could have earned two. Shough Stopper is the opposite case: the league's highest points for (921.82, about 46 more than anyone else), five top-five weeks, and second place.

Week 7 reshuffled the middle. Team Stowell's second straight W+T5 week moved it from 8th to 6th. Team Lackey went from tied for second to fourth after losing and missing the cut by 5.86. Team Whitehead had the league's best score (148.08) and moved from 9th to 7th. Ranks 4 through 7 are separated by two points, and 8th and 9th are only one more behind. Two max weeks would move almost anyone in that group into the top three.

The cut line has ranged from 109 to 131 points. Four of seven weeks were decided by less than three points between fifth and sixth. With two weeks left in the round robin (both points still live), every team is still playing for a weekly maximum of two.

Scoring format note: Weeks 8 and 9 finish the round robin (matchup win plus top five). From Week 10 the matchups stop counting and each weekly top-five finish earns two points. From then on, the head-to-head opponent doesn't matter, only the score.

The moves, in order

1. Add Jonah Coleman (RB, Broncos): bid $22, drop J.K. Dobbins

Conviction: high on the move, medium-high on winning at this price · Expected competition: Team Jensen (owns Harvey, has $75, has paid $15), maybe Stowell or Carnahan at single digits · $1 wins? No

This is the swap the Week 7 snap counts make for us. In Coleman's first game back from an ankle injury that cost him Weeks 3 to 6, he played 50% of snaps, with 9 carries, 6 routes and 2 targets for 69 total yards. RJ Harvey played 25% and Dobbins 21% (6 carries, 23 yards and a touchdown that saved his fantasy day). Dobbins refused interviews afterward. FantasyPros ranks Coleman as the #1 add this week with a $25 value. Remember that Coleman was the league's top add back in Week 3 (This Sucks paid $12, then dropped him three days later when he got hurt).

Coleman's upside is a lead back in a good offense; Dobbins' realistic outcome now is the 6-carry backup who needs a touchdown to matter. Swapping them is the cleanest upgrade available, with the same Week 10 bye.

Why $22: the league's #1 add has gone for $15 to $18 for four weeks running, with a $40 and a $51 outlier. Jensen is the bidder to beat: they own Harvey, they have $75, and they beat Justin on ties. Their largest bid is $15. His Airdness is capped at $15, and Carvalho at $32 hasn't bid on anything since dropping Wilson and Pierce. $22 clears every observed habit with room, and leaves $60 for the last ten weeks. I wouldn't go above $30: if Harvey keeps the passing downs, Coleman is a two-down back.

If he loses: keep Dobbins one more week and see whether Week 7 was the new normal. If Coleman's share holds, Dobbins goes for Tyson in Week 9 instead of Andrews.

2. Add Emanuel Wilson (RB, Seahawks): bid $8, drop Keaton Mitchell

Conviction: medium for Week 8, low for the rest of the season · Expected competition: Team Stowell (need, $6 max so far), This Sucks ($3 cap) · $1 wins? Maybe, but not worth risking

Wilson had 24 carries, 100 yards and a touchdown against Kansas City (18.5 points). Earlier he scored 27.0 in Week 4 when he had the lead role. Zach Charbonnet, coming back from ACL surgery, was inactive in Week 7 despite practicing fully. Mike Macdonald calls it a structured plan, and on Monday he wouldn't commit to Charbonnet playing against Chicago. If Charbonnet sits again, Wilson is a 20-carry back in Week 8. If Charbonnet returns, Wilson becomes a committee back, which is why this is a one-week rental and the bid stays small.

Why Justin needs it: Barkley might not play, and if he does it will be on Sunday night, after every other Justin RB has kicked off. If Barkley is ruled out Sunday evening, the only replacement is a player who hasn't played yet. Wilson's game vs. Chicago kicks off Sunday afternoon, well before Barkley's, so he can't be a late swap for him. What he does is make it cheap to bench Barkley before the afternoon games if Barkley hasn't practiced fully by Friday, without falling back on Dobbins. Carvalho dropped Wilson, so they won't bid. Stowell needs RBs, but their largest bid is $6. $8 goes past that.

If he loses: Rico Dowdle (Pittsburgh, 6 catches and 14.6 points in Week 7, about 44% of snaps) should be free Wednesday morning. But Pittsburgh is off in Week 9, so he's a one-week rental too.

Mitchell is the drop. He is third in the Chargers' backfield and has scored 0.4 and 5.9 in his last two games played. His rest-of-season projection is the lowest on the roster.

3. Add Hunter Henry (TE, Patriots): bid $3, drop Bo Nix

Conviction: medium-high · Expected competition: low; no team has a TE hole · $1 wins? Probably, but $1 loses ties to five teams

Kittle's bye was always going to be covered by Andrews, but Andrews has stopped earning the start. His last three games were 4.8, 1.4 and 5.3 points. He played 57% of snaps in Week 7, after Baltimore got a blocking tight end back, with under 15% targets per route two weeks running. Henry is going the other way: 7 catches on 7 targets for 71 yards on Thursday, and 13.4, 14.8 and 14.1 points over his last three games. NFL.com ranks him #4 on its wire. He plays at Miami in Week 8, a better matchup than Andrews' trip to Buffalo.

After Week 8, Henry is a better TE2 behind Kittle than Andrews, who is still coming back from an Achilles. That makes Andrews the obvious Week 9 drop when Tyson comes off IR.

Nix is the drop: Allen is back, and he hasn't missed a game. A 10-team league with one QB slot always has a streamer on the wire, which is why carrying a backup is a waste of a spot (Justin's stated preference).

If he loses: Chig Okonkwo (Washington; NFL.com #3, absorbs McLaurin's targets) or Mason Taylor (Jets, 25.2 points in Week 7, though that was two touchdowns on 5 catches) should be free Wednesday. Otherwise start Andrews.
