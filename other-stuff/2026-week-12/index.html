The cut line came back down to 112.36, after 130.36 in Week 10. The scores split into two groups. The middle of the table is now close together. His Airdness has won both 2-point weeks and moved to 14, 1 point behind Carvalho (15). Stowell sits 5th at 12. Behind them, Carnahan and Lackey have 10, Whitehead has 8 and Cee Deez Nuts has 7. Ceiling still beats floor. Team Whitehead's Week 11 top-five came almost entirely from Jonah Coleman (34.9) and two WRs over 16. The rest of the lineup was ordinary (Allen 12.2, Taylor 6.7). That is how this format pays out: one spike player in the FLEX is worth more than three steady 10-point starters. When a lineup decision is close, start the player with the bigger range of outcomes.
