What the table says

Team Jensen is the only team that has taken every available point: 3–0 with three top-five weeks, for 6 of a possible 6. Their lowest score, 110.80 in Week 2, still cleared the cut line. That is the profile this format rewards. Breaking Burrow is also 3–0 but missed the top five in Week 1, and that single miss is the whole one-point gap between them.

Week 3 split the league into two groups. Five teams scored 125 or more, and the other five all scored 103 or less. A 23-point gap between fifth and sixth is the widest of the season by far. Last week the cut was decided by 0.32 points. This week, nobody in the bottom half came close. When the top five clears 125, lineup decisions at the margins stop mattering. What decides the point is having a ceiling that can reach 125.

The middle of the table is crowded. Four teams are tied at 3 points: Cee Deez Nuts (1–2), Team Carvalho (2–1), Team Stowell (2–1) and Team Whitehead (1–2). Two of them got there on wins and two on top-five finishes. Only the points-for tiebreaker separates them. Cee Deez Nuts had the best week of the four, going from 1 point to 3 by winning and finishing top five in the same week. That double is the fastest way up the table in this format.

His Airdness has the sixth-most points for in the league (349.82), within a point of fifth, and is ninth with 2 points. They scored 150.12 in Week 1 and have not reached the cut line since. Team Carvalho's path looks similar: 153.00 in Week 1, then 108.70 and 88.86. One big week goes into the points-for total, but it earns at most one top-five point.

Scoring format note: through Week 9, every team plays every other team once and both points are live (matchup win plus top five, maximum two per week). From Week 10 the matchups stop counting and each weekly top-five finish earns two points.

The short version

  1. Close the Week 7 quarterback hole: Geno Smith, $4. Drop KC Concepcion. This has been open for three weeks, and Week 7 is now three weeks away. Geno faces Miami at home that week. He also covers Josh Allen's bruised knee this week.

Justin's best possible lineup would have scored about 117. That is still short of Breaking Burrow's 133.90 and the 125.84 cut line, so no standings point was lost. The miss was a real error of process, though: the call treated a "does not expect to play" report as settled when it was not.

The moves, in order

1. Add Geno Smith (QB, Jets): bid $4, drop KC Concepcion

Conviction: high on the need, medium on the player. 7.1% rostered · 9.3, 15.6, 26.0 this season · NYJ bye Week 13 · Week 4 at CHI, Week 5 vs. CLE, Week 7 vs. MIA

This page has recommended a second quarterback three weeks in a row, and none was added. Goff went for $10 while Justin passed. The issue is now more pressing for two reasons. Allen's knee bruise is very likely nothing, but it is a reminder that Justin's QB depth is zero. And Week 7 now takes four starters, not three.

Why Geno over the bigger names: the Week 7 schedule decides it. Geno gets Miami at home in Week 7, a defense that just lost its best offensive player and is playing from behind. C.J. Stroud (home against the Winston-led Giants) is a close second. Sam Darnold threw for 379 yards and 4 TDs but has Kansas City in Week 7. Aaron Rodgers is at New Orleans. Bo Nix's completion rate is around 50% under a new coordinator. Geno also has the best schedule of the group over the next three weeks: Chicago's banged-up secondary, then Cleveland. FantasyPros ranks him the top streaming option this week.

Expected competition: Bijan Mustard is the only manager with a QB need, for his Week 5 bye. His QB bids so far have been $1, $1 and $3, and he tends to chase the name national columns are pushing, which is Darnold or Stroud. $4 clears every QB bid he has made. If he takes Geno anyway, Stroud or Brissett is very likely free on Wednesday morning. Do not overpay.

The drop: Concepcion caught 2 of 9 targets for 9 yards on about 24 snaps, and plays Thursday in the lowest-total game of the week. Of the seven active receivers, he has the lowest ceiling, and Tyson's return in mid-October will force one receiver out anyway. Drop him now for a need that is certain.

2. Add Ollie Gordon II (RB, Dolphins): bid $9, drop Tank Bigsby

Conviction: medium. 1.6% rostered · Week 3: 61 of 73 snaps, 17 carries for 41 yards and a TD, 3 catches on 3 targets · MIA bye Week 6

Achane's torn ACL leaves Miami's lead-back job open for the rest of the season. Gordon played 84% of snaps in the first game without him. Jaylen Wright (foot and stinger) will return and take some of that work, and Gordon has averaged just 2.7 yards per carry for his career. He is not a star. But a waiver running back who is on the field for more than half the snaps every week, catches passes, and sees goal-line carries is rare. He would replace a back who played 2 snaps.

Why Gordon over Braelon Allen, the national #1: Allen's value lasts only as long as Hall's quad injury, reported as one to two games. Gordon's opportunity lasts the rest of the season. For a team that is not desperate this week, the longer runway is worth more. Gordon also covers the Week 10 bye, when Barkley and Dobbins are both out.

Expected competition: real. Stowell has the most urgent RB need in the league and has bid before, but his high is $6, and Justin wins ties against him. Bijan will probably spend on Allen, the consensus #1, the way he did on Coleman last week. His Airdness has the strongest motive, since Gordon replaces their own player, but has never placed a claim. $9 beats any bid Stowell has made and wins a tie against him or Carvalho. It loses only if Bijan or His Airdness goes to $10 or more. If Gordon goes for $12 or more, let him go.

The drop: Bigsby was added as Barkley insurance, and Monday night showed he is not. Barkley played through the stinger, and Shipley was the backup when it mattered. Bigsby is a waste of a roster spot.

3. Pass on Braelon Allen (RB, Jets)

Conviction: medium. 14.3% rostered · CBS 20%, FantasyPros $15 · handled all the RB work late in Week 3 after Hall's quad injury

He will start at Chicago and is a sensible one-week RB2. The problem is timing and price. The MRI on Hall was described as good news, with one to two games the likely absence. Three teams have motives: Bijan (the #1 add), Stowell (RB need) and Carvalho (Hall's owner). This is the one player this week likely to go for $12 or more, for perhaps two weeks of production. Last week's version of this call was to pass on Jonah Coleman at $12. He was on IR and off Bijan's roster within four days.

4. Hold Brian Thomas Jr. one more week

Conviction: low. 37% of snaps in Week 3; Washington and Meyers at 81%

Thomas's usage is the most worrying thing on this roster. But one game is not a trend, and Jacksonville plays Cincinnati in the highest-total game of the week (51.5). If he is under 50% of snaps again on Sunday, he becomes next week's drop, especially since he is also out in Week 7. Do not start him this week.

Drops, justified

  • Tank Bigsby (first). He was added only as Barkley insurance, and the Monday snap counts show he is not the insurance. Nothing is lost by dropping him.
  • KC Concepcion (second). Nine targets is a real signal, but 24 snaps and a 2-for-9 day in Cleveland's offense is a weak foundation. When Tyson comes back, a receiver has to go anyway. This moves that drop up to cover the QB need.
  • Do not drop Rachaad White. He out-snapped Croskey-Merritt and has scored 14.3 and 11.1. The Week 7 bye is a cost, not a reason to cut him.
  • Do not drop Brian Thomas Jr. yet. See move 4. He gets one more week.
  • Do not drop Mark Andrews unless the hand injury turns out to be significant. He still covers Kittle's Week 8 bye.

How confident to be in all this

  • The quarterback move is the most certain call on the page. The need is fixed on the schedule. The only question is which quarterback, and at least four good answers are available.
  • The Gordon bid is a judgment call about price. $9 is the league's going rate for a contested add, and Justin now wins ties against the two teams most likely to be at that number. If His Airdness bids for the first time, the logic above is wrong and that is fine. Mitchell or Johnson free on Wednesday is a reasonable fallback.
  • Allen's knee is the one to watch. Every source says bruise, and he did not miss a snap. One aggregator listed a concussion, which appears to be a data error. If Allen does not practice by Thursday, having Geno on the roster matters right away.
  • These bids are visible to the whole league. This page is shared with the league. Anyone who reads it before 1:00am MT Wednesday can see the Gordon number and outbid it by $1. That is Justin's call.
