What the table says

Team Jensen has finished in the top five six weeks out of six. No other team has more than four. That is why Jensen leads by three points while sitting third in points for. Jensen has never had the league's best week; it just never has a bad one. In a format that pays for ceiling, steady has still been enough, because the cut line has never been higher than 131.

Week 6 was a low-scoring week, and the cut line fell to 110.86. Team Carnahan missed it by 0.26 points, after Shough Stopper's lineup squeaked in at 110.86. That is the third time in six weeks the gap between fifth and sixth was under three points. Shough Stopper has the most points for in the league (775.54) and is tied for second, behind a team that has scored 22 fewer points.

Three teams are tied at 8 points behind Jensen. Shough Stopper, Team Carvalho and Team Lackey are separated only by points for. Carvalho is 5–1 but has only three top-five weeks; Lackey and Shough are 4–2 with four each. Below them, five teams sit between 4 and 6 points, so one good week can move a team three or four places.

Low weeks are where points are cheapest. In Week 6 a 111-point lineup earned a top-five point. In Week 5 it took 131. Nobody can predict which kind of week it will be, so the only lineup rule that holds is the one this table keeps showing: when two options are close, start the one with the higher ceiling.

The moves, in order

1. Add C.J. Stroud (QB, Texans): bid $3, drop Terry McLaurin

Conviction: high on the need, medium-high on Stroud over the alternatives · Expected competition: none likely · $1 wins? Probably

Stroud's last four weeks: 11.7, 23.1, 20.9, 23.0. He threw for 312 yards and three touchdowns against Jacksonville and has averaged about 307 yards and two touchdowns over his last three. Week 7 is a home game against a Giants team that just benched its quarterback, which usually means a favorable game script. ESPN projects him at 19.2, essentially tied with Aaron Rodgers (19.5) for the best free QB this week. I prefer Stroud for the role: Rodgers is playing in Paris against New Orleans, and he finished with 16.0 last week. Jaylin Noel has given him a deep threat, with 85, 41 and 107 yards in his last three games.

Why bid at all when nobody needs a QB? Because this is a mandatory slot, and a $3 claim locks it in before the Wednesday-morning free-agent rush. This Sucks has made most of the league's early-Wednesday adds, and starts Bryce Young. The only cost is three dollars Justin isn't likely to need.

If he loses: add Aaron Rodgers or Michael Penix Jr. (vs. San Francisco, 23.1 points last week) for free Wednesday morning. Kyler Murray (home vs. Indianapolis, 18.0 projected) is also free; he faces Jonathan Taylor's team, which slightly hedges the matchup.

McLaurin is the drop because his season is over. His ESPN rest-of-season projection is 0.

2. Add Omar Cooper Jr. (WR, Jets): bid $16, drop Keaton Mitchell

Conviction: medium on the player, medium on winning at this price · Expected competition: His Airdness (likely $15), Team Stowell and Team Lackey (need, but no bidding history) · $1 wins? No

Why it fits Justin's roster: McLaurin's injury leaves five healthy receivers for two WR spots and part of two FLEX spots, and three of them (Odunze, Washington, Concepcion) are projected at about 9 to 10 points. Cooper projects in the same range now, but he has the most room to grow, which is what the cut line rewards. The Jets' bye is Week 13, the same week as Flowers and Taylor, so plan around that later.

Why $16: the consensus #1 add in this league has gone for $15 to $18 in each of the last three weeks. His Airdness bids $15 by habit, and Justin wins a tie with them, but $16 avoids relying on that. Stowell (largest bid ever, $6) and Lackey (no claims ever) both need a receiver but have not shown that they bid. $16 leaves Justin $63 for the last ten weeks, which is still the fourth-largest budget in the league. I would not go above $20: Cooper is a FLEX upgrade, not a lineup fix.

If he loses: no replacement bid is needed. Ryan Flournoy (Dallas, 17.0 points and the team lead in routes in Week 6) and Jaylin Noel should both be free Wednesday morning.
