What the table says

Week 10 was the first 2-point week, and the top five got richer. All five teams that cashed in (His Airdness, Carvalho, Stowell, Chuban Missles and Jensen) were already in the top six of the table. Nobody from 7th down gained ground, so the chasing pack (Lackey 10, Carnahan 8, Cee Deez Nuts 7, Whitehead 6) all fell 2 more points behind 5th place. Lackey had been tied with His Airdness and Stowell at 10.

The cut line jumped to 130.36. That's the second-highest of the season (Week 5 was 130.68), up from a 112.48 median through Week 9. His Airdness put up 179.92, the second-best score of the season after Chuban Missles' 187.18 in Week 8. This Sucks scored 126.94, its best week of the year, and still finished 6th and missed by 3.42. Head-to-head results are now meaningless. Team Whitehead beat Lackey 99.70–88.90 and got nothing for it, while This Sucks lost and came within one big play of 2 points.

Chuban Missles is the real favorite, not the leader. Jensen leads by 2 (17 to 15), but Chuban Missles has 1,379.48 points for, 129 more than Jensen and 158 more than anyone else, and it has made the top five in eight of ten weeks. Over a seven-week scoring race, raw points per week is what wins. Jensen's lead came partly from matchup wins (seven of them) that no longer pay.

For everyone from 6th down, it's about ceiling, not floor. Fourteen points remain. Team Whitehead is 6 behind 4th and 5th (His Airdness and Stowell, both at 12), so three more top-five weeks than they get closes the gap. A steady 110 now finishes 6th or 7th most weeks and pays nothing. The lineup choice that matters is the one with the higher upside: two-way backs with goal-line work, and receivers who see deep targets, over safe 9-point floors.

0. Required: drop Hunter Henry, then move Tyson from IR to the bench

Conviction: high · no FAAB · do it before claims process at 1:00am MT Wednesday

Tyson has been off injured reserve since Week 9 and has played two games, scoring 5.0 and 19.0. As long as an ACTIVE player sits in the IR slot, ESPN treats the roster as illegal and won't process a claim. This also cost you 19 points in Week 10, when Tyson outscored White by 12.8.

Why Henry. He's on bye this week, he's scored under 7 points in three straight games, and Kittle has no bye left. If Kittle gets hurt, streamers like Michael Mayer (if Bowers stays out) and Chig Okonkwo are available for $0 in this league. Do the drop first: moving Tyson to the bench needs an open roster spot.

1. Add Seth McGowan (RB, IND) — bid $13 — drop Keaton Mitchell

Conviction: medium · about 11% rostered on ESPN · Yahoo's suggested bid: $7 · bye Week 12 (same as Taylor) · plays Thursday

The role. The Colts kept Taylor at the deadline but didn't go back to giving him every carry. In Week 10, McGowan had 12 carries and the goal-line TD, his second straight game with a touchdown. NBC's Pelissero expects Taylor at about 70–75% of snaps from here. Yahoo's read is that a 3–7 team could shut Taylor down at the first minor injury. Either way, McGowan is the back who inherits the job, and right now he's taking goal-line work from your RB1.

Why it fits this roster. In a top-five race, the worst case is losing Taylor and replacing him with White's 6-to-10-point floor. McGowan turns that into a starting back. Even with Taylor healthy he's a better bench back than Mitchell, who scored 0.4 in Week 10. He also gives you a handcuff that plays Thursday, so if Taylor is ruled out late, you'll know before the Sunday games.

Who else bids. Carvalho dropped McGowan on Nov. 11 to add Kaleb Johnson, and then nobody took him as a free agent for four days, even before his Week 10 TD. That's real evidence of low demand.

  • Team Jensen ($59, waiver #10) is the only real threat. Kenneth Walker (groin) is questionable, Kiner and Stevenson projected 0 this week, and Jensen has paid $15 twice (Hill, Kiner). Justin wins a tie with Jensen, so $13 beats any Jensen bid of $13 or less, but loses to its usual $15. That risk is accepted rather than chased.
  • His Airdness ($15, #4) has six RBs, including Montgomery and Emmett Johnson (35.1 in Week 10). Unlikely.
  • Lackey ($100, #2) needs RB help (Henderson IR, Monangai Q) but has never placed a bid in eleven weeks.
  • Stowell ($98, #3) and Carnahan ($74, #7) are deep at RB. Their top bids all season are $6 and $10.

Why $13, about double the national price. FAAB is worth nothing in Week 18. With seven weeks left and $70 in the bank, the cost of overpaying is small, and losing Taylor without the handcuff would hurt. Only a handful of winning bids this season have topped $13 (Allen $51, Montgomery $40, Allgeier $22, Marks $18, Gordon $17, and a few at $15). It still leaves $57 for a real injury replacement later. If you want to beat Jensen's habit outright, $16 does it, but that's paying for a backup.

If it loses: nothing changes. Keep Mitchell, and don't chase other backs.
