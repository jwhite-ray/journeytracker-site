Who else bids, and how much. This is the league's consensus #1 add (SI: "THE top Week 9 waiver add"; ClutchPoints #4 behind Coleman, who's already on this roster). Why $33 and not $20. $20 probably beats everyone except an all-in Carvalho, and Carvalho is the one team with both the need and a habit of overpaying. Losing a tie on $32 is the scenario that hurts. After $33 you have $37 left with eight weeks to go, which is still the 5th-largest budget in the league. If you'd rather keep more FAAB, $26 is the lowest bid I'd make: it beats every team's demonstrated habit except Carvalho going nearly all in. Below $20, expect to lose him.

Why Mitchell is the drop. He's been the drop candidate for four straight reports: third in the Chargers' committee, and 5.3 points in a week the starters were healthy. He has no Week 10 value beyond a bodies-in-slots role, and Allgeier fills that slot better.
