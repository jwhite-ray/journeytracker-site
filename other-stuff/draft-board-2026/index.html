2026 Draft Board

A weighted consensus of 13 expert ranking boards (ESPN, PFF, Yahoo, FantasyPros, Draft Sharks, CBS, and more), collected the week of the draft. Dif is consensus rank minus ESPN's rank — negative means the room drafting off ESPN will probably let them fall to you. Pts/Wk is projected points per week over the best bench option at the position. Green ★ steal rows are potential steals — players projected for at least 1.2 more points per week than the typical player drafted around them. Mark players Mine or Taken as the draft unfolds — progress is saved on this device until you hit Reset.

Missing values (—) mean that source doesn't rank the player. Built from a weighted average of 13 boards with accuracy-based weights and midpoint imputation for unranked players. 2025 keepers are excluded from the pool: Cam Skattebo, Jameson Williams, DeVonta Smith, George Pickens, Javonte Williams, Chris Olave, Caleb Williams, and Tyler Warren.
