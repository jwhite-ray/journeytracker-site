JourneyTracker

Real walking, real journeys.

JourneyTracker turns the walking you already do into progress along real-world and original fantasy journeys. Your walking and running distance — read from Apple Health with your permission — moves your marker along the route, past waypoints, all the way to the end.

Support

Questions, problems, or feedback? Email journeytrackerapplication@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Your privacy

JourneyTracker has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics — your data stays on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
