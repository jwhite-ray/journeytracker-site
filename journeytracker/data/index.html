Map Data

JourneyTracker's real-map view draws surveyed trail geometry. Three of the bundled trail files are derived from the OpenStreetMap database — © OpenStreetMap contributors — and are published here as Derivative Databases under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:

  • laugavegur.track.json — Laugavegur Trail, Iceland (OSM relation 1225037)
  • cinque-terre.track.json — Cinque Terre coastal path, Italy (OSM relations 7798145, 7797964, 7797939, 7795328)
  • inca-trail.track.json — Camino Inca, Peru (OSM relation 15703494)

Each file is an extracted, stitched and simplified trail centerline keyed by journey leg. You are free to use these files under the ODbL's terms. The app's other trail files are derived from public-domain US Government sources (NPS/USFS/USGS) and carry no license obligations.
