1. What JourneyTracker is

An app that turns walking distance from Apple Health into progress along real-world and imagined journeys. It is not a navigation app, not a medical device, and not a fitness or health product. Distances from Apple Health are estimates and can be wrong. Don't rely on anything in this app for a decision about your health, your safety, or where you are.

2. Using the app without an account

You don't need one. Without an account everything runs on your device, nothing is sent to us, and nothing is backed up. If you delete the app or lose your phone, that data is gone — that's the trade, and it's the reason accounts exist.

3. Accounts

Accounts use Sign in with Apple. You're responsible for the Apple ID you sign in with. You need to be at least 13, or at least 16 in the EU, EEA and UK. If you're under that age, don't make an account — the app works fully without one.

4. Your backup

When you're signed in we keep a private copy of your journeys so they survive a new phone. It's private to your account and we don't show it to anyone. We try hard not to lose it, but we don't guarantee it: keep in mind it's a convenience, not an archive of record. You can export a copy any time.

5. What you share

Sharing is off unless you turn it on. Settings > Sharing has two switches — Journeys & progress, and Trophy case — each set to Everyone, Trailmates, or Not shared, and both start at Not shared. They apply to your whole account, not to individual journeys. With Journeys & progress on, your journeys' progress and your display name can be shown to other people in the app, a finished journey's time can be shown and — once fastest-finishes lists exist — can appear there, and your distance counts toward weekly and monthly totals. Everyone includes public leaderboards; Trailmates does not. Set a switch back to Not shared and everything it had shared is deleted, across every journey; totals already counted stay counted. Your display name is shown wherever you're visible and can't be hidden on its own.

You give us permission to show your display name inside the app to people who can see your shared progress — that's all we do with it, and only for as long as you keep an account.

7. Playing fair

Don't fake your progress. Don't feed the app distance you didn't walk, don't tamper with the data we receive, don't automate or script the app, and don't try to read other people's data or work around the limits we put on how often the app can send. We check submissions that don't look physically possible and we reject them. Persistent attempts to cheat can cost you your account.

8. JourneyTracker Premium (subscriptions)

JourneyTracker is free, and stays free: every journey in the catalog, Trailmates, trophies and maps, with one journey moving at a time. JourneyTracker Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription that lets you keep up to three journeys moving at once.

Plans and price. Premium comes in monthly and annual plans. The price is set through the App Store, shown in the app in your local currency before you buy, and charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.

Renewal and cancellation. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Apple charges the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can cancel any time in your device's Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next renewal and Premium keeps working until the period you've paid for runs out. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

If your subscription ends, you lose nothing you've done. Every journey that is already moving keeps moving — progress, maps, notifications and trophies all keep working. The only thing that changes is that you can't set more journeys moving than the free allowance until you subscribe again.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Price changes. If a price changes, Apple notifies you in advance under its rules, and where required asks you to agree before the new price applies.

Complimentary access. Some accounts — early testers, friends of the project — have Premium features enabled at no charge. Complimentary access is ours to grant and withdraw; withdrawing it never takes away a journey that is already moving.

License. The app itself is licensed to you, not sold, under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement; these terms apply on top of it.

We review every report within 24 hours and act on it — a forced rename or a suspended account. Questions or an urgent report: support@wjamm.com.
