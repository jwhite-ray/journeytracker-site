Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

The short version JourneyTracker has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Your data stays on your device.

Health data

With your permission, the app reads walking + running distance (moves your marker) and step count (extra stat only) from Apple Health. Read-only — never writes, never shares. Processed and stored entirely on your device. You can change access in the Health app under Sharing, or Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

What the app stores

Journeys, progress, and reached waypoints in a private on-device database. Nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

If allowed, notifications are scheduled locally (e.g., reaching a waypoint). No push server.

What we don't do

No accounts, analytics, ads, third parties; no data leaves your device.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything goes with it. Your Apple Health data is unaffected.

Changes

If a future version changes data handling (e.g., optional iCloud sync), this policy updates first.

Contact

Questions about this policy? See the support page.
