Common questions

My progress isn't moving.

JourneyTracker reads walking + running distance from Apple Health. Check that access is granted in the Health app under Sharing, or in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > JourneyTracker. Progress counts distance recorded since the moment you started each journey.

Does the app track my location?

No. JourneyTracker never uses GPS or your location. Your position on a journey map is computed from distance alone — the map is the story, your steps are the fuel.

Where is my data stored?

On your device — and, only if you sign in, in a private backup on our servers so your journeys survive a new phone. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Your privacy

JourneyTracker works with no account and no internet. If you sign in, we keep a private backup of your journeys; sharing anything with other people is a separate, opt-in choice that starts switched off. Read the full privacy policy and terms of service.
