Every step moves you

Your walking and running distance — read from Apple Health with your permission — carries your marker along the route, past waypoints, all the way to the end. No workouts to log, no GPS, nothing extra to do. Just walk.

Journeys worth taking

Walk historic routes across real geography, or set out across original fantasy lands. Each journey has its own map, its own waypoints, and its own story unfolding at walking pace.

Private by design

No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Your health data is read on your device and stays there. Read the privacy policy — it's short, because there's not much to disclose.
